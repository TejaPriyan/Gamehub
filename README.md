## ⚡ The Vision

Teja Priyan GameHub was born out of a love for retro arcade thrills, synthwave neon aesthetics, and lightning-fast web performance. 

Too many modern web games are cluttered with long download bars, intrusive popups, and clunky controls. GameHub strips all of that away:
- **Zero Downloads & Zero Waiting**: Instant start in any modern browser.
- **Pure Canvas Performance**: Native 60–120 FPS rendering loops with responsive pixel scaling.
- **Zero Scroll Interference**: Custom anti-scroll event listeners keep arrow keys, Spacebar, mouse wheels, and touch swipes strictly inside the game — your page will never jump around while you're dodging lasers.
- **Hardware-Level Audio Synthesis**: Powered directly by the Web Audio API without bulky audio assets.

## 🎮 The 8 Cyber Mini-Games

Each game in the arena is built from the ground up on its own custom canvas loop with responsive DPI scaling, high-score memory, dynamic difficulty, and tactile visual cues:

### 1. 👾 Cyber Invaders: Neon Strike
A fresh cyberpunk reimagining of the iconic space shooter. Defend the mainframe against descending rows of glitch aliens, use destructible energy bunkers for tactical cover, and shoot down high-speed mystery UFO motherships for massive bonus scores.

### 2. 🏍️ Neon Lightcycle: Grid Surfer
Channel the legendary digital grid. Pilot your high-speed lightcycle, laying down impassable neon laser walls while cutting off a smart AI competitor. Features turbo boosts and high-density mainframe grid arenas.

### 3. ⚡ Neon Pulse (Hyper Rhythm Runner)
A 5-lane hyper-speed reflex dasher. Collect cyan and gold data cores to charge your **OVERDRIVE** meter, dodge lethal red security barriers, and trigger hyper-speed bursts when you reach 100% power.

### 4. 🚗 Quantum Velocity (Highway Drift)
Tear through the digital neon highway at breakneck speeds. Weave through traffic, pick up nitro fuel cells, and drift across lanes with realistic acceleration and headlight beams.

### 5. 🌀 Vortex Defender (360° Perimeter Turret)
Command a rotating center turret and hold the line against incoming multi-directional cyber swarms. Accumulate charge to trigger a room-clearing EMP blast when enemies close in.

### 6. 🧩 Hexa Matrix (Neon Plasma Breaker)
Ricochet a glowing plasma orb off your responsive bottom paddle to shatter colored data nodes. Features multiballs, angle-of-incidence trajectory physics, and particle explosions.

### 7. ⚔️ Cyber Slash (Laser Blade Defender)
Slice through airborne data anomalies and energy projectiles before they hit the ground. Test your reflexes with multiplier combos and particle trails.

### 8. 🏃 Grav Runner (Gravity Flip Corridor)
An endless runner where the laws of physics are your weapon. Tap Space or click to invert gravity between floor and ceiling, avoiding spike grids and collecting quantum energy shards.

## 💎 Arena Features

- **🖥️ True Full-Screen Arcade Mode**: Hit **`[F]`** or click the Fullscreen button at any time. Features automatic canvas aspect scaling and viewport optimization so the player character and controls are always 100% visible and centered.
- **🪪 Holographic Operative Dossier**: Build and customize your player identity (Codename, Gamer Tag, Cyber Role, and Avatar). Your stats, tier, and high scores are tracked locally and showcased right inside the game HUD.
- **🕹️ Multi-Input Support**: Full support for Keyboard (Arrows / WASD / Spacebar), Mouse clicks, and on-screen touch virtual controls for mobile operatives.
- **🎵 Real-Time Web Audio Synth**: Custom oscillator engines simulate laser shots, explosions, engine revs, overdrive alarms, and victory chimes on the fly without downloading external audio files.
- **✨ Cyberpunk Visual Polish**: Tailored scanlines, CRT glow effects, particle sparks, dynamic shadows, and Framer Motion transitions.
