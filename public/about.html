About Gamehub

What this project does, who maintains it and where to find help.

Updated 27 October 2026

What you can do here

Gamehub is a browser arcade created by Teja Priyan with eight canvas-based games and a player-card creator. You can practice lane changes, timing, aiming and grid navigation without installing a separate game. Scores saved in your browser are personal records, not a verified global leaderboard.

Practical guidance

The guides explain common tasks and limitations. They are written for this project’s current behavior, rather than promising that every browser or device behaves identically. If an instruction does not match what you see, report the page and browser through Contact.
